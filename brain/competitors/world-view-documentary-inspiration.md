# World View - Documentary: what to borrow for The History Invoice

Date: 6 November 2026. Source: vidIQ channel data + 8 thumbnails + the transcript of "Iran vs Israel & USA: The Origins of The Conflict" (1h47, 1.28M views). Reference only: mood and structure. Nothing is copied.

## 1. What the channel is

- Re-licensed broadcast documentaries (French/European producers, English re-voice). Suffixes in titles (AMP, AT, EM, JV, MDW, OTF) are the licensors.
- 50 min to 1h50 per video, 44.6k subs, about 1.65M views in 30 days, subs up 778% in a year. Growth comes from the titles and thumbnails, not from the format: the format is 20-year-old TV.
- Footage: real archive, news agency reels, on-camera interviews (ex-ministers, officers, historians), maps only as short inserts.
- Top videos: Iran vs Israel & USA 1.28M, JFK 772k, Cambodia 1975 712k, Chicago gangs 590k, Gulf power struggle 571k, North Korea 398k. Geopolitics with a villain or a secret does best; same lane as ours.

## 2. Title pattern (strong, borrow it)

Thumbnail text and YouTube title are two different lines:

| Thumbnail text (3-4 words, two lines, second line on a red block) | YouTube title (long, keyword-rich) |
|---|---|
| 40 YEARS OF **CONFLICT** | Iran vs Israel & USA: The Origins of The Conflict |
| THE SECRET **GULF WAR** | The Gulf Power Struggle: ... |
| KIM JONG-UN **SECRET STRATEGY** | The Kims' Nuclear Trap ... |
| 600 GANGS, **ONE CITY** | Chicago gang wars ... |
| KHMER ROUGE **TAKEOVER** | Cambodia 1975 ... |
| AFGHANISTAN **USSR'S TOMB** | ... |

Words that repeat: Secret, Inside, Untold, Trap, Origins, Hidden. Always a number or a superlative in the thumbnail.

For us: keep the wallet stake in the YouTube title (under 60 chars) and put a 3-4 word "secret / number" line on the thumbnail. Example for Pickaxe Mountain:
- Thumb: ONE STRIKE, **YOUR BILL** or PICKAXE **MOUNTAIN**
- Title: What a Nuclear Strike on Iran Does to Your Bills

## 3. Thumbnail formula (borrow the layout, not the faces)

- Left 60%: one strong scene (missile parade, crowd, soldiers on a ridge), desaturated and darkened.
- Right 40%: one big face, cut out, sharp, lit, looking at the camera or just past it.
- Text bottom-left, white bold caps, second line white on a red block. Channel badge top-left.
- No explosions, no gore, no mock news UI. This already fits our YouTube policy table.
- Our version: same layout but the face is a leader photo we have rights to (government/public-domain portraits, Wikimedia with licence noted) or a silhouette, and the red block becomes our orange.

## 4. Opening structure (the first 2 minutes)

From the Iran transcript:
- 0:11 thesis in one line: "the war that dares not speak its name ... never officially declared".
- 0:27-0:58 stakes: "the entire Middle East would go up in flames ... a new global conflict".
- 1:00-2:07 title sequence and music (a minute of nothing; we never do this).
- 2:07 the story starts on one person on one day: "February 1st, 1979, aboard a flight from Paris to Tehran. An old man prays."
- 3:40 first eyewitness interview.

Borrow: a one-line thesis, then a dated scene with one person. That is our Round Two rule already (money by 0:30), so the fix is: thesis line, money number, then the dated scene by 0:45.

## 5. Pacing and voice

- Narration is slow, formal, literary ("the tangled threads of tragedy"). 12,000 words in 107 min = about 112 wpm with long music gaps.
- Interviews carry the emotion; narration carries the facts. Every 2-3 minutes a human voice other than the narrator.
- Music cues sit under every transition, and the picture goes quiet for 3-5 seconds at chapter turns.

Borrow:
- Quiet beats: at each part end, 2-3 seconds with music only and one still, before the question card. Our cuts never breathe.
- Other voices: we have no interviews, so use dated, attributed quotes read as typed text (already our style) and public-domain audio where it exists (US government briefings, UN sessions, DVIDS).
- Keep our speed (Kokoro 0.92 is about 135 wpm). Do not slow to theirs; our audience is phone viewers.

## 6. Footage mix (the biggest gap between them and us)

They are 90% real footage, 10% graphics. We are 90% graphics, 10% footage. The rebuild v4 already moved toward footage behind cards. Target for the next video: about 50/50, using only:
- Public domain: DVIDS, NARA, US Navy/Air Force imagery, NASA, UN Web TV (check licence per clip), Library of Congress, Wikimedia Commons CC.
- Stock: Pexels/Pixabay clips Hammad downloads from the coded B-roll list.
- Never: broadcast footage, news agency reels, clips from other YouTube videos.

## 7. Chaptering

Their long docs are built as 5-7 acts, each about 15 minutes, each ending on a turn ("It was the only way to protect ourselves"). Ours are 18-20 minutes in 4-5 parts, so one act = one part, each ending on a question (engagement rule). Match.

## 8. What not to copy

- Length (50 min+). Our cadence is one 18-20 min video every other day.
- Minute-long title sequences and logo cards before the story.
- Their footage and their licensed interviews.
- Real-person thumbnails of living leaders where we have no licence for the photo.
