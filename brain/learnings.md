# Learnings

Rules we follow because they were learned (by us or in the handbook). Newest first. Each line: rule · why · source/date.

## Seed rules (from handbook ch.12 and competitor scan, 2026-11-01)
- Show the finished result in the first 5 seconds and on the thumbnail · high CTR + drop at 0:15 means the intro broke the promise · handbook ch.12
- Thumbnail: subject one side, tension/result other side, 2-4 words · low CTR fix · handbook ch.12
- A visual change every 4-5 s, subtle motion on stills, real SFX, burned-in captions · avoids "reused/inauthentic content" YPP strikes · handbook ch.12
- Animate a still keyframe (image-to-video) instead of text-to-video for host clips · less warping · handbook ch.12
- Keep captions above the bottom 20% and clear of the right 10% on Shorts · platform icons cover them · handbook ch.12
- 10 ms crossfade at every audio cut · removes pops · handbook ch.12
- New channel: warm up the Google account 3-5 days (browse, like, subscribe in the niche) before first upload · zero impressions otherwise · handbook ch.12
- Tool-test titles that win right now: "I Tested X", "How to Make X With Claude + Y", "FREE" in title · competitor scan 2026-11-01
- Sponsored "I tested X" mills (TryThisAI, Liqui AI) get flat ~15-25K views; their outliers are 3D/creative tools with a visible result · competitor scan 2026-11-01
