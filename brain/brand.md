# Brand: The History Invoice

## Promise
We take the crisis everyone is watching and follow it until it lands on your bill: fuel, food, rent, savings. Money is one line on the invoice, never the only one: lives, health, home, food, water, land, jobs, time, rights, heritage, trust, safety.

## Lane (a topic must pass this first)
Geopolitics, war, sanctions, a choke point, a power struggle or the history of one, that can be followed ring by ring to an ordinary household. Out: partisan domestic politics, money basics, celebrity news, pure military gear.

## Signatures
1. **The Receipt** closes every video: an itemised household bill per month (US, UK, South Asia when relevant). Running corner invoice prints one line per part.
2. **Confidence tags** on every on-screen number + a short source line.
3. **Rings:** the thing, the line, the region, the money, your table.

## Voice
One calm, measured narrator (Kokoro, `am_onyx:0.5,am_michael:0.5`, speed 0.92, about 132 wpm, -16 LUFS). Sober, empathetic, never hype. Clear neutral American English. One idea per sentence, most under 20 words. Evergreen wording: no "today / this week"; every number carries month and year.

## Look (locked in `docs/video-process.md`)
- Colours: ink black (16,15,14), paper (236,228,214), orange (232,89,12). Weapons red #E63946, water cyan #00B4D8, SPENT crimson #C1121F, warning orange #FF5400.
- Fonts: Inter Bold headlines, Caladea Bold Italic orange second line, Bebas for stamps.
- Footage grade: desaturated, cool, slightly dark, bottom-left shade under text. Grain + vignette on every frame.
- Maps: dark terrain hillshade (AWS terrarium DEM) + Natural Earth borders, country in orange.
- Thumbnails: one map or object, one glowing element, 2-3 words of stakes that never repeat the title.

## Never
Gore or bodies; AI faces of real people; fake documents or invented quotes; lines or titles copied from Paycheckology or any channel; stock or crypto picks; greeting or logo before the first story line; banned phrases in `docs/master-prompt-v3.md` section 6.
