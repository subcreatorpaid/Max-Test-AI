# INV-009 Hook lab (Stage H)

Scored 1-5: pays off the title in 30 s · concrete image in 5 s · no greeting/channel name · fact-pack numbers only · makes the viewer ask a question. Rulebook: search phrase "what happens if China invades Taiwan" by 0:15; household money number + 2 s receipt flash by 0:30.

**Winner: H1 + H3 merged** (the ships for the 5-second image, then the household number by 0:30). **Runner-up: H5** is the Shorts opener ("Used cars +45%, and Taiwan was fine"). H4 is Short 2.
