## Five unknowns (honest beats)
1. Whether Beijing intends anything in 2027 at all: US intelligence (F26) and US Army Pacific (F28) disagree in the same year.
2. Blockade, quarantine or invasion: the costs differ by a factor of two (F44-F46).
3. Whether the US would fight: Washington keeps it deliberately vague; experts aren't confident (F38).
4. What the 100+ ships in November 2026 were doing: Taiwan's numbers, AEI/ISW's reading, Beijing silent (F31-F34).
5. How fast chip plants outside Taiwan can take over (Arizona, F08): years, not months.
