## Unknowns (say so on screen if used)
1. How much tantalum is in one phone: not found in a reliable source. Do not give a milligram number.
2. Whether Ebola has moved coltan or gold prices: no evidence found. Gold actually fell to a one-year low on 12 Nov 2026 ($3,868/oz, Khaleej Times/Reuters) for other reasons. The honest ring is war + minerals keep the outbreak alive, not "your phone got dearer".
3. True case count: suspected and community deaths are outside the confirmed totals (CDC note); real toll likely higher.
4. Exact patient zero and spillover date: unconfirmed.
5. How much of the $2.9bn pledged came from which donors, and how much of the US $112m was spent: not found.

## Story note for storyteller
Bill to the viewer = three real lines, no invented price spike: (a) the minerals in your devices come from the same ground the armed groups fight over, and that fight is why health teams cannot reach the sick; (b) the cheap fix (the unreleased $1.6bn) vs the expensive bill West Africa ran up ($53bn); (c) travel rules, aid money and the risk that reaches you (ECDC: very low). Keep tone calm, no fear-mongering.
