# Story

- **Question:** Can Claude actually edit my YouTube video, or just give advice?
- **Stakes:** editing eats a whole evening per video; an editor costs $30-100 per video [CHECK].
- **Arc:** result first (the finished edit, side by side with the raw mess) -> doubt ("AI can write, but it can't touch my timeline") -> Level 1 (easy win) -> Level 2 (real time saved) -> Level 3 (the stress test: something breaks) -> fix -> before/after -> verdict.
- **Open loops:**
  1. 0:15 "By the end you'll see if Level 3 beats a human editor. It didn't go the way I expected." (closed at verdict)
  2. 2:30 "Claude spotted something in my raw footage I completely missed." (closed in Level 1)
  3. 5:00 "This is where it broke." (closed with the fix in Level 3)
- **Max moments:** hook (confident), Level 1 surprise (surprised), Level 3 break (sceptical, laughing), outro (smiling).
- **Payoff:** a clear score per level + exact time saved (stopwatch on screen).
- **Next:** "The host you're watching? I'll show you how I made him consistent in Google Vids."
