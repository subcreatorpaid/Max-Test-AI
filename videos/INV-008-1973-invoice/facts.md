## Five unknowns (honest beats)
1. How much of the 1970s inflation was oil, and how much was already in the pipe (F50): economists still argue.
2. Whether the 2026 price spike fades like 1974 or returns like 1979 (the second shock).
3. Whether today's reserves (SPR at a 1982 low, F57) could handle a second disruption.
4. The exact US gasoline series (F14 is a widely cited figure; check BLS/EIA monthly before lock).
5. Whether Britain's 1974 crisis was oil or coal: both, and we say so (F29-F32).
