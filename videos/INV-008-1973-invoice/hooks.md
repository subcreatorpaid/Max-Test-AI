# INV-008 Hook lab (Stage H)

Scored 1-5: pays off the title in 30 s · concrete image in 5 s · no greeting/channel name · fact-pack numbers only · makes the viewer ask a question. Rulebook: search phrase "1973 oil crisis" by 0:15; household money number + 2 s receipt flash by 0:30.
