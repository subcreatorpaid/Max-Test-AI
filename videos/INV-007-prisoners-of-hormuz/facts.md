## G. Unknown or disputed (honest beats)
1. **How many are still trapped:** IMO said ~20,000 (Mar-Jun); DP World ~6,000 on up to 400 ships (late Oct). Different counts, different dates, different areas.
2. **Is traffic back?** IMO PortWatch: 1 transit on 1 Nov. Trading Economics: shipments close to pre-war by 10 Nov. Both can be true for different ships (escorted Saudi tankers vs everyone else).
3. **Who hit which ship:** many attacks are unattributed; Iran claims mines, the US struck ships too (F24).
4. **The death toll:** IMO 25 vs Iran's union 44+ Iranian seafarers (CLAIM, May).
5. **Whether the Qatar talks (Trump meets Qatar's PM, 9-10 Nov) reopen the strait:** Iran's two-day plan vs Rubio: "not realistic".
