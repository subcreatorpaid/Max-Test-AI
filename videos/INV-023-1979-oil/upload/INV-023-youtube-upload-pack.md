In late 1978, oil workers in Iran went on strike and output fell from about 6 million to 1.5 million barrels a day. World supply fell only about 4%. But the panic was huge: queues at American pumps, odd-even rationing, oil from $15.85 to $39.50, 22% inflation in Britain, 20% interest rates in America, and a recession that put millions out of work. We follow the 1979 bill instalment by instalment, and what it tells us about the bill from Iran in 2026.
