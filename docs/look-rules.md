# Look rules (Hammad's picture notes, living file)

**Every director/animator run reads this before a storyboard or render.** Each time Hammad gives a picture note,
add it here the same day (newest at the bottom of its section, dated), update `scripts/storyboard2/kit*.py`, and
note it in `brain/learnings.md`. His notes win over the reference analysis and older docs.

## Footage and photos
- Every clip/photo must match what the narrator says at that moment. No random or generic filler; if nothing matches, use a graphic. Fetch 4 candidates per shot and pick by eye (2026-11-10).
- Colour, like INV-006, not grey-toned (2026-11-10).
- About 30% of footage time in SHADOW (dark, near-colourless, cold, heavy vignette) on sombre beats: deaths, abandonment, the people left out. The rest colour-graded by scene intensity: calm (cooler, softer), normal, intense (punchier, warmer) (2026-11-10). Map: `GRADE` in kit.py.
- All colour shots 20% darker than the source (brightness x0.8) (2026-11-10).
- Never show or say "Illustration" on screen (2026-11-10).

## Graphics
- Style: our brand mixed with the Round Two draft reference (paper cards, stamps, split-flap, receipt printer, cut-paper figures). Studied at 3 fps (2026-11-10).
- Every graphic sits on the part-card background: dark ledger with faint lines. Maps too (2026-11-10).
- Paper cards are beige (214,204,186), never white (2026-11-10).
- No magnifier / microscope / lens. Documents get a highlighted row and a red pen circle (2026-11-10).
- No particle-assemble effect. End card is plain type that fades in (2026-11-10).
- A different graphic type every time. Repeats only for: chapter (part) cards, the Receipt, and graphics for the same explanation (e.g. the same map, the same checklist) (2026-11-10). One-off types live in `scripts/storyboard2/kit2.py` (31 types); invent new ones per video rather than reuse.
- Never invent data to fill a chart: show only numbers from the fact pack.

## Mix and sound (from INV-006)
- About 50% graphics / 30% video / 20% Ken Burns stills; smooth crossfades; music bed throughout; SFX sparse; 1080p only; about 30 min.

## Process
- Storyboard in picture form (PDF) goes to Hammad for OK before any render. Version up (v1b, v1c...) with each round of notes.
