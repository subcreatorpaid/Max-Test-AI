**Three problems to fix in the script itself: (1) the payoff comes after most viewers have left, (2) home-feed viewers leave at about 4.5 minutes, (3) nobody can find the video by searching.**

# 2. Rules we keep (unchanged)

- Evergreen wording: no relative time words ("today", "yesterday", "this week", "this weekend"). Every number carries its month and year.

- Every number comes from the fact pack with a tag: VERIFIED / REPORTED / CLAIM / ESTIMATE / SCENARIO. Never invent precision.

- Concentric rings: the thing, the line, the region, the money, your table. The Receipt closes the video.

- Length 18-20 minutes (about 2,400-2,800 words at our voice speed). Cut, never pad.

- A household money number inside the first 30 seconds, plus a 2-second flash of the final receipt as a promise.

- Nothing before the first story line: no logo, title card or disclaimer card. The disclaimer goes in the description or a small lower-third.

- Never copy lines or titles from Paycheckology or any other channel. No paper textures, no decorative stamps.

# 3. New rules: search (so people can find us)

- **Pick the search phrase first.** Before writing, choose one phrase people really type, checked in YouTube search autocomplete (for example "Strait of Hormuz", "Iran war gas prices", "what happens if Iran"). Write it at the top of the script.

- **Title formula.** Search phrase in the first 40 characters + the wallet stake. Under 60 characters. Example shape: "Strait of Hormuz: What a Closure Costs Your Family". Words only insiders understand ("Round Two?") never stand alone.

- **Say it out loud.** The narrator says the exact search phrase in the first 15 seconds and once in every part. YouTube reads the captions.

- **Chapters are searches.** Name chapters the way people search: "How Hormuz sets the price of gas", not "Act 1: The Water".

- **Description.** The first two lines repeat the search phrase in a full sentence and say what the viewer will learn. Then chapters, then sources.

- **Evergreen angle.** Pick a title that still makes sense in 6 months ("what happens if...", "why...", "how X reaches your bill"), and keep the news peg inside the video.

- **Tags and file.** 3-5 tags (phrase, country, "gas prices", "oil prices"), and name the upload file with the search phrase.

- **Thumbnail complements the title.** Thumbnail text never repeats the title: 2-3 words of stakes plus a map or object.

# 4. New rules: hook and pacing (to keep home-feed viewers)

| **Time**        | **What must happen**                                                                                        |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 0:00-0:05       | One strong image + one stake line. No greeting, no channel name.                                            |
| by 0:15         | The search phrase is said.                                                                                  |
| by 0:30         | A household money number, and a 2-second flash of the final receipt.                                        |
| by 1:00         | The promise in one line ("by the end you will see every line on this bill") + the roadmap in one breath.    |
| by 4:00         | FIRST MONEY PAYOFF: a real number for the viewer (moved earlier: home-feed viewers leave at about 4.5 min). |
| 4:00-4:30       | Subscribe ask, 10-15 s, tied to the next video (moved earlier: the average viewer leaves before 9 min).     |
| middle (~9 min) | Half-receipt: the running total so far, so the biggest drop point gets a reward.                            |
| last 2 min      | The Receipt + 4-6 practical steps + one comment question + end screen to another video.                     |

- Viewer touchpoint every 60-90 seconds: bring it back to "your bill" with a number or a household object.

- Every part ends with a specific question the next part answers. Never "but that's not all".

- Running invoice in the corner: one line prints at the end of each part.

- Pace changes: mix fast 3-second cuts with 2-3 slow, quiet moments. Nothing longer than about 90 seconds at one speed or in one visual system.

- History and background sections are capped at 2 minutes each and must end on money.

# 5. Rules for after upload

- Do not change the title or thumbnail in the first 24 hours.

- Run Test & Compare with 3 thumbnails from day one.

- Pin one comment question and reply to every early comment.

- Post 1-3 Shorts from the video over the next week, each with Related video set to the long video.

- Read the data at 24 h, 7 days and every 3 videos, and update this rulebook.
