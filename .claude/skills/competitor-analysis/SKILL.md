# competitor-analysis

Per channel (vidIQ; check balance first, ~20 credits a channel):
subs, upload rhythm (long videos/30d), average length, top 10 videos by views-vs-median, title patterns, thumbnail patterns (`similar_thumbnails`), hook style (first 30 s from `video_transcript` of 3 top videos), pacing, visual style, host type, music/SFX, monetisation (sponsors, affiliates, products), comment complaints (`comment_insights`) and gaps.
Write `brain/competitors/<channel>.md` (sourced and dated), then update `landscape.md` (what everyone does, what nobody does, our angle) and add ideas to `brain/ideas.json`.
Copy structure ideas only, never content.
